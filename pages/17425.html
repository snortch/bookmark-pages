When bartering, convert to your local currency in your head and work out what you would be happy to pay. That way both you and the souk owner should be happy. Many pairs of colorful, decorative flat shoes are displayed on a white pegboard wall in what appears to be a market stall or shop.

Don't exchange money at the airport, instead use a cash machine to withdraw dirhams or use the currency exchange in the medina for the best rates. Most riads offer a transfer from the airport and normally accept euros as payment.
