In Morocco, the greatest recipes are traditionally kept secret, passed down quietly from the Dadas, the women who have always been the true keepers of Moroccan cuisine. This restaurant exists to change that. To bring those recipes out of hiding and share them. Book ahead. This place fills up fast and does not apologise for it. Sit at the bar if you can.
