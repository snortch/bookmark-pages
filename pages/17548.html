What You're Building You're building a cold-email writer that critiques and rewrites its own drafts until they hit a quality bar. A first model writes the email, a second cheaper model scores it 0-10 against a fixed rubric and says what to fix, and the system loops that feedback back into a rewrite. When the score passes the bar (or it runs out of allowed attempts), a third, stronger model gives a final human-style verdict. The real-world use case is anything where first-draft quality is not good enough and you can describe what "good" looks like: outreach emails, support replies, product descriptions, ad copy. The same loop works for code that must pass tests or summaries that must hit a length.
