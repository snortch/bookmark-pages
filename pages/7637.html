Chinas Clandestinos started popping up in Lisbon in the early 2000s. They would be located in family homes, no signage, and definitely no Google listing. The only way you would know they existed would be by looking up and seeing red lanterns in the window. A makeshift dining room would be set up in the living space with kids' toys that might be tucked away in the corner. Someone from the family would take your order, and then another family member would cook your food in the household kitchen. And if you were lucky, one of the kids would be your server. Because these restaurants were essentially illegal, a big raid happened in the mid-2000s where Lisbon police shut down about half of the city's Chinas Clandestinos.
